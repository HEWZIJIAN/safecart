Privacy you can verify

Your data stays on your device

SafeCart was built privacy-first. The sensitive work happens inside your browser — not on our servers.

Read the full privacy policy →

  • Gmail reads stay in your browser

    Receipt matching uses your own Gmail token, on-device. No email content ever reaches our servers.

  • No API keys in your copy

    Phishing checks route through a thin proxy, so your extension never carries third-party keys.

  • Breach checks send only domains

    We look up public store domains like "example.com" — never your email, password, or anything that identifies you.

  • No ads, no trackers, no sales

    There are no analytics SDKs in the extension and we never sell or share your data. Full stop.

Frequently asked questions

Everything else you might be wondering before you install.

Is SafeCart really free?
Yes. Phishing detection, purchase tracking, daily breach scans, and chargeback help are free forever, with no ads. Premium ($2.49/mo) and Family ($4.99/mo) are optional and only add Gmail receipt matching, faster breach alerts, and cross-device sync.
Can SafeCart see my card number or passwords?
No. SafeCart never reads or transmits your card details or passwords. It detects when a card field is present so it can warn you on risky pages — but the numbers you type never leave the page, and card payments for subscriptions are handled entirely by Stripe.
Will it slow down my browsing or flag real stores?
No. SafeCart returns a fast local verdict first and caches results, so there's no noticeable lag. A curated allowlist of well-known retailers keeps legitimate stores from being false-flagged.
What data actually leaves my device?
Very little. Phishing checks send the checkout URL to a privacy-preserving proxy; breach checks send only public store domains. Purchases are stored locally. Gmail receipt-matching (Premium) runs in your browser. You can read the exact details in the privacy policy.
Which browsers are supported?
SafeCart runs on Google Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers (it's built on Manifest V3). Microsoft Edge support is on the way.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Open the SafeCart popup, go to your subscription, and click "Manage subscription" to open the Stripe billing portal. You can cancel anytime and keep all the free features.
