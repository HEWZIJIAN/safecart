Welcome to SafeCart

Your shopping safety net for the internet.

SafeCart watches over you while you shop online — in three ways. Let's take a 30-second tour.

🛡️

1. Phishing Protection

Stop entering your card on fake stores.

When you land on any checkout page, SafeCart checks the URL against Google Safe Browsing, looks at how old the domain is, and detects lookalike scams. If something's off, you'll see a red warning before you type your card.

⚠ Dangerous amaz0n-checkout.xyz looks like a phishing scam
💳

2. Payment Tracking

Never lose a receipt again.

Every time you click "Buy" on a real store, SafeCart logs the purchase automatically. See all your online purchases in one place. If something goes wrong, start a chargeback in one click.

✓ Tracked Amazon — $42.99 — Jan 15
🚨

3. Breach Alerts

Know within hours when a store you shop at gets hacked.

SafeCart watches the stores you've visited against the Have I Been Pwned database. If your data leaks, you'll get a notification with exactly what was exposed and what to do next.
